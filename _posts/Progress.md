So my very first "big" project, though it was an assignment, is a ping pong SDL clone. It's very finicky and not quite ping pong.

I called it that because I had an idea of it. That project isn't great — to me it was an accomplishment.
I spent hours on this code, felt like I made a game, and was (and still am) proud of it ╰(▔∀▔)╯.

Now I'm learning Vulkan, feel very confident with OpenGL and SDL, and have written more projects since my first semester.
A year after "ping pong" I participated in midstone project where we made a playable proto game in SDL.
After midstone, my big project was an ECS engine in OpenGL where I played around with cameras and physics — mostly collisions.
Now I've been learning Vulkan for about a month. It's fun but hard. I like it (⁀ᗢ⁀)
